# Soft Signal

Soft Signal is a visual language built around an invitation: a small, quiet input can move an entire room. The identity should feel like a darkened living room after sunset—calm enough to disappear into the screen, vivid enough to be found at a glance.

The central form is a contained touch surface with a single point of contact. Its response travels outward as measured arcs, not noise. Make the relationship between touch and motion legible through spacing, curve, and alignment; let the eye finish the gesture between the marks.

Color behaves like light reflected from a display rather than paint on a wall: deep ink as the stable field, warm coral where a choice becomes active, violet for depth, and a restrained mint for confirmation. Gradients should be smooth and intentional, with the precision of a tuned instrument—not a generic neon wash.

Scale is intimate at the center and expansive at the edges. Keep the mark compact and balanced, but let its signal have room to breathe. Rhythm comes from repeated radii, soft corners, and deliberate pauses, as if every interface motion had been reduced to its clearest trace.

Words remain few. Typography is a quiet frame around the symbol, never a substitute for it. Every curve, join, and interval should feel meticulously considered and refined through repeated passes: the finished identity is the product of patient craft, not decoration applied at the end.
